## 🔬 Featured Technical Research & Projects

### 1. [BIOHUB-Cell-Tracking](https://github.com/ayushshuklaivyleague-jpg/BIOHUB-Cell-Tracking)
**4D Volumetric Embryonic Lineage Tracing from Anisotropic Light-Sheet Microscopy**
- Formulated an un-strangled 3D mitotic geometry model and logit-calibrated neural assignment for large-scale cell tracking in embryonic volumes.
- Increased macro Division Jaccard by **+78.0% relative** ($0.3125 \to 0.5563, p = 0.03125$) across held-out embryos.
- Achieved **0.91348** on the official Chan Zuckerberg Biohub blind evaluation server.
- `Python` · `PyTorch` · `Integer Linear Programming (SCIP/ilpy)` · `Zarr / Blosc2` · `Computer Vision`

### 2. [Gallstone-research-](https://github.com/ayushshuklaivyleague-jpg/Gallstone-research-)
**Predicting Ultrasound-Detected Gallstones from Non-Imaging Clinical Markers**
- Rigorous retrospective machine-learning study on cross-cohort generalization across CDC NHANES III ($N = 12,824$), UCI Turkish Clinic ($N = 319$), and modern NHANES cohorts.
- Uncovered the *Silent Gallstone Paradox* (88.5% of active ultrasound gallstones unperceived by participants) and demonstrated empirical domain collapse from tertiary hospital models to community screening.
- `Python` · `Scikit-Learn` · `XGBoost` · `LightGBM` · `PyTorch` · `Biostatistics`

### 3. [RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection](https://github.com/ayushshuklaivyleague-jpg/RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection)

- `PyTorch` · `Medical Image Computing` · `DICOM` · `Probit Fusion` · `CUDA / AMP`
